# Research decision records

Decision records explain why Makata chose a research question, model, or method. They are supporting documentation, not evidence of peer review or institutional approval.

- [0001 — Tagalog reading comprehension](0001-tagalog-reading-comprehension.md)
- [0002 — Baseline model selection](0002-baseline-model-selection.md)
- [0003 — Evaluation protocol](0003-evaluation-protocol.md)
- [0004 — Exploratory Aya adaptation](0004-exploratory-aya-adaptation.md), recorded retrospectively October 13, 2026.

These first three records were written retrospectively on October 12, 2026. They distinguish earlier committed criteria from locally retained configuration evidence. None establishes public preregistration.

For a new decision, use the next number and record: title, recorded date, status, context, problem, documented alternatives, selection criteria, direction, rationale, constraints, evidence, consequences, and revisit conditions. Do not backdate a record or invent deliberations. Cite the relevant source revision or evidence hash. Preserve the original decision when amending it and explain the amendment.
